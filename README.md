# Conduit

**Your internal people network** — a directory for your organization so you can find alumni and members without the noise of a public social feed.

## What it is

Conduit helps people inside an organization discover who is who: backgrounds, roles, education, and experience, in one place built for _your_ org instead of the open web.

## Who it is for

Teams, schools, alumni groups, and member communities that want a shared, searchable roster of people — with sign-in and flows tailored to joining a specific organization.

## What you can do

- **Join your organization** through the guided onboarding flow (organization access, identity, and optional profile claiming).
- **Search and explore** people in your org to understand skills, companies, schools, and roles.
- **Save people** you want to come back to.
- **Open profiles** for a fuller view of someone’s path and how they describe their work.

## Privacy and data

Profile and directory data are scoped to **your organization**. Treat the directory as internal: share only with people who are meant to have access, and follow your org’s policies for personal data.
